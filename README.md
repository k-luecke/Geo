# Geo — Schumann Resonance & Geophysical Timing Research Notebook

Open research notebook preserving the exploratory analyses, preregistered hypotheses, negative results, and closure ledgers from the Driftbound Labs Schumann-resonance and geophysical common-view timing lineage.

> **Why keep the full notebook public?** A research program and a curated reproducibility package are different objects. `Geo` preserves the complete evolution of the question — including branches whose stability criteria failed (`D2b`) and post-result sensitivity checks — while [`k-luecke/time-integrity`](https://github.com/k-luecke/time-integrity) isolates the curated six-hour LIGO Hanford/Livingston O3 reproducibility package.
