Time Integrity from a Shared Geophysical Signal:
Cross-Node Correlation and Sequential Detection
of Delay Attacks
Kyle Luecke

Abstract—Two-way time transfer is rank deficient: one exchange measures the sum of the forward and reverse path
delays, but only their difference enters the offset estimate, so an
adversary delaying one direction by δ shifts the estimated offset
by δ/2 while every packet stays cryptographically authentic.
We close the gap with a globally excited, spatially correlated
geophysical signal, the 6 to 25 Hz Schumann band, recorded
independently at each node and exchanged inside authenticated,
acquisition-timestamped records, so that the timing fiducial rides
in message content and out of reach of transport delay. Fitted
mode parameters on a four-year record give a 90 s integrity
floor; the raw waveform supports 12 to 363 µs at assumed unity
coherence. We then replace assumption with measurement. Public
magnetometer records from observatories 3002 km apart show
fundamental-mode coherence that survives a pre-declared null
battery and disjoint-epoch replication, bounding offset precision
at 184 µs per day through a facility sensor noisier than the signal.
A matched-sensor test, frozen and hash-deposited before the data
were seen, confirmed its preregistered prediction, with coherence
rising fifty-fold inside a forecast window of thirty to one hundred,
for a measured 76.6 µs bound at six hours and a projected 38 µs at
one day. A separately preregistered lag-stability criterion was not
met: the lag is detected in nearly every window but wanders with
source geometry, a measured limitation retained for the named
follow-on rather than relaxed. Against slow attacks,

I. I NTRODUCTION
ACKET-BASED time transfer under IEEE 1588 (PTP) secures message integrity but not message timing. Section II
shows the vulnerability is algebraic, not implementational: the
two-way exchange cannot separate path asymmetry from clock
offset, so a delay attack forges nothing and breaks no key.
Four known remedies exist, being a second disjoint path, a
holdover clock, an engineered-reciprocal medium, and out-ofband radio time. Each assumes a resource some nodes lack;
Section II-C states the condition each one requires and the
theorem that condition traces to. The unserved node is singlehomed, carries a commodity oscillator, and sits where GNSS
is denied, jammed, or indoors.
The architecture analyzed here needs four properties of
its reference signal, none specific to the Schumann band:

B. The Vulnerability is Demonstrated, Not Hypothetical

D. State of the Art

Barreto et al. built a hardware delay box that advances or
retards a slave clock by a few microseconds, and reported
it undetectable both from the protocol’s perspective and by
the slave’s clock-servo algorithm, independent of the cryptographic protection in use [3]. Finkenzeller et al. subsequently
demonstrated static and incremental variants against PTP at
microsecond precision on a testbed [7].
Encryption does not close the gap. Annessi et al. showed
that statistical traffic analysis identifies PTP messages inside
IPsec-, MACsec-, or TLS-protected traffic well enough to
support selective delay, and identified the underlying conflict:
the deterministic traffic pattern that makes a synchronization
protocol precise is the same pattern that makes its packets
identifiable for selective delay [4]. Obfuscation trades precision for concealment in both directions.

The state of the art has not moved off these preconditions.
PTPsec [8] is the most complete treatment. It entangles
detection with synchronization by invoking a measurement
packet on every PTP event message, derives cyclic roundtrip asymmetry over redundant paths, integrates into IEEE
1588-2019 [10], and detects both static and incremental attacks
on hardware within a few synchronization intervals. Its own
stated condition is that redundant paths be available in the
network, and the authors note this is already enforced by
standards such as TSN, which is precisely the deployment
class the unserved node of Section I is not in. The 2025 journal
extension reinforces the point rather than relaxing it [9]: it adds
software-defined networking for dynamic topology discovery, redundant-path management, and deterministic routing,
machinery whose necessity is itself a measure of what the
redundancy precondition costs to satisfy. The 2012 conditions
therefore still bound the current art; what has changed is the
quality of the defense available to nodes that can already meet
them.
Patching PTP cannot repair rank deficiency. That the missing information must come from outside the protocol is not
a new claim: Barreto et al. stated it as the conclusion of the
delay-box work [3], and Annessi et al. reached it independently [4], adding that knowledge of one-way delays and of
the physical constraints of the network is what would permit
a guaranteed bound on slave offset. This paper contributes
not the diagnosis but a specific exterior: a reference that is
globally available, requires no infrastructure, is not carried by
the network, and admits a measured rather than assumed bound
(Section IV). In the terms above, the claim is an enlargement
of SB , not a better element of it.

C. Packet-Domain Defenses and Their Preconditions
Mizrahi’s game-theoretic treatment makes the boundary of
the existing design space explicit [1]. An attacker chooses
insertion delays (δM S , δSM ); a defending process decides, at
the end of each exchange, whether to accept or discard it. The
defender’s strategy set is SB = {f | f : R4 → {Pass, Drop}},
the space of functions of the four timestamps and nothing else.
That definition, rather than any particular f , is what the present
architecture contests. Round-trip thresholding, multiple clock
sources, and path redundancy are three choices of f ; all three
read arrival times, and (1) is the statement that arrival times
do not contain the missing rank.
Each choice of f carries a sharp precondition, summarized
in Table I.
The threshold condition is Mizrahi’s: a drop threshold
DRT admits an equilibrium only when 2εmax ≤ DRT ≤
∆ERR + 2εmin for delays confined to [εmin , εmax ]. That is,
only when the accuracy requirement exceeds twice the delay
spread, which is satisfiable in a low-variance single-hop setting
such as an 802.1AS bridged network but not in the wide-area
case. Path redundancy inherits N > 3m from Lamport and
Melliar-Smith [2], and the accompanying bound on residual
error of the surviving paths is, by its author’s own statement,
not strictly a formal claim but rather an intuition.
PTP is weaker here than NTP. Round-trip detection depends
on time distribution and delay measurement being coupled
within one exchange, as they are in NTP; PTP does not
guarantee that coupling. Under the peer-to-peer variant the
Pdelay handshake is independent of the Sync messages that
carry time, so an adversary who delays only Sync leaves
the measured path delay untouched. Under end-to-end, the

E. Alternative Reference Signals
Read against R1–R4, the candidate exteriors sort as follows. Mains phase is regional and fails R1 across interconnects, demonstrated incidentally by the grid-based null
in Section IV-D. Geomagnetic pulsations and the millihertz
geomagnetic art [21] lack bandwidth for sub-millisecond work
and fail R2. Sferic time-difference-of-arrival methods [22]
require per-event association and share the source-geometry
systematic without the continuous integrability of the resonant
band. GNSS is the out-of-band remedy whose denial defines
the unserved node.
The Schumann band [18], [19] is not argued to be unique.
It is the candidate that currently survives all four filters, and
the architecture accepts any reference that does.

3) Error budget: Statistical terms divide down with event

4) Coherent forgery at scale: Reproducing the cavity realization at one victim sensor requires a local field matching
the global one in band. Doing so consistently across geographically separated verifiers requires either planetary-scale
source power injected into a cavity of quality factor Q ≈ 4, or
physical capture of every sensor, at which point the adversary
has capabilities against which no timing protocol is a defense.
Forgery of the record is the MAC’s job; forgery of the global
field is priced by physics.

5) Local injection: The preceding argument prices global
forgery and does not dispose of a local one. An adversary with
physical proximity to a single sensor can receive the in-band
field and re-radiate a delayed copy into that sensor’s near field.
The natural field is 0.1 to 1 pT in band, so the power required
is not exotic; the requirement is proximity, not scale.
Such an injection is not merely undetected. Adding δ at the
sensor of node A adds δ to τ̂AB , which is indistinguishable
from θAB having shifted by δ. It is precisely the signature
the verifier is built to read as a clock offset, so the monitor
would issue a correction in the adversary’s favour. Nor does
adding nodes help: an injection at one node perturbs every pair
through that node identically and therefore cancels in any loop
sum, so a three-node closure test is blind to it by the same
rank deficiency that (1) describes. A causal repeater can only
delay and never advance, but the constraint binds per node
rather than globally: (3) is antisymmetric in A and B, so an
adversary who can choose which site to reach can produce
either sign.
Four discriminants remain, and they are partial. Amplitude,
since a re-radiator must dominate the natural field locally
rather than merely add to it, and a partial-amplitude injection
sums with the real field and appears as structure in the crossspectrum. Near-field polarization geometry, since a local loop
presents a field structure that a multi-axis sensor sees differently from the cavity’s horizontal-mode structure, which is the
same physics as the vertical-coil null of Section IV-D. Ramp
detection, since a growing injection is what Section III-D is
for. And sustained physical access.
A static, well-matched injection installed at commissioning
defeats all four. The honest statement of what the architecture
achieves is therefore a relocation rather than an elimination: a
remote on-path network adversary is converted into an on-site
radio-frequency adversary, with a correspondingly different
and considerably more expensive access requirement. That is
a real security improvement and it is not the same claim as
unforgeability.
6) Division of labour: Forge the global field, and sourcepower physics prices it. Forge the record or its timestamp,
and authentication catches it. Replay, and sequence numbers
catch it. Delay in transport, and correlation reads content
rather than arrival. Creep below a threshold, and Section III-D
bounds the gain. Inject locally, and the residual above stands.
Cryptography and physics each cover the column the other
cannot.

E. Instrument
A single-axis induction coil with a low-noise preamplifier,
sampled at a few hundred hertz against a GPS-disciplined or
holdover-disciplined ADC clock, suffices to record the band.
The vault magnetometers used below represent the low-noise
end of the same commodity class. Sensor self-noise enters (4)
only through γ 2 (f ).
F. Data
The Gravitational Wave Open Science Center (GWOSC)
auxiliary-channel releases [15], [16] include magnetometers
at the LIGO Hanford (H1) and Livingston (L1) observatories,
3002 km apart, with GPS-disciplined timestamps. They constitute an accidental two-node testbed. Schumann-band content
in these channels is independently documented in the detectorcharacterization literature [13], [14].
The pilot uses the GW170814 three-hour auxiliary release:
2.63 h of coincident data in two disjoint epochs on 201708-14. Livingston provides a low-noise vault magnetometer
of the class deployed for Schumann monitoring; Hanford, in
that release, provides only facility fluxgates whose self-noise
exceeds the cavity signal several-fold. The pairing is a worstcase sensor test, and every pilot coherence reported below is
a lower bound on what matched sensors achieve.
The follow-on uses the O3 auxiliary release, which carries
vault magnetometers at both sites, at 4096 Hz via NDS2.

The O3 analysis was frozen before any O3 sample was

1) The two precision quantities: A reader holding Sections IV-E and IV-C has two numbers differing by three orders
of magnitude, and their relation must be stated rather than
inferred.
στ is the geophysical monitor’s own offset-estimation precision at integration time T : the resolution with which an
independent verification layer bounds a node’s offset, and
therefore the smallest static or step displacement it can certify
against. θdet is the bias a ramping adversary accumulates
before the sequential detector alarms on the servo’s offsetinnovation sequence. The constant κ is calibrated on the fouryear record, but the σ in (5) is the servo innovation scale,
which is why θdet is reported in nanoseconds and στ in
microseconds. The monitor sits beneath the servo rather than
in place of it: the servo supplies precision, and the monitor
bounds how far that precision can be displaced without the
displacement becoming observable.
2) Transfer of κ: Two limits are stated here rather than
deferred. By Proposition 1, κ is an alarm threshold in signalto-noise units, so at fixed false-alarm rate it depends on the
autocorrelation of the innovation sequence and not on its scale
alone. The calibrating record has a measured spectral exponent
of 0.107, so κ is fixed under mildly correlated innovations
and applied to a sequence treated as white. Because positive
correlation reduces the effective independent-sample count,
this transfer inflates κ relative to its white-innovation value
and renders θdet conservative in that respect.

B. Independence: Physical Against Topological
R1–R4 govern reference signals. The nearest alternative
architecture is path redundancy, which is not a reference at
all, and the comparison turns on what its independence rests
on.

7

Both approaches introduce a second information source
against which the primary channel is checked. Path redundancy’s independence is topological and assumed: it requires
the adversary to occupy at most m of N paths, and disjointness
at the logical layer does not imply disjointness in physical
plant. Shared conduit, a shared transit autonomous system, or
route manipulation can defeat it, and the slave generally cannot
verify that they have not. The independence claimed here is
physical: the cavity field is not carried by the network under
any topology, so no arrangement of adversary position or route
control brings it within reach.
Provisioning costs differ accordingly, being more than three
verified-disjoint long-haul paths against one sensor per node,
and so do the failure modes. Redundancy degrades silently
when its disjointness assumption is false, whereas loss of
cross-node coherence is observable at the node.
C. Limitations
1) Measurement campaign: The measured coherence
comes from one baseline, three epochs totalling under nine
hours, and two sensor classes. Both nodes are instruments
of a single observatory network with homogeneous dataacquisition chains, so a commodity node of independent
construction remains the outstanding independence test. Multibaseline and multi-season coherence maps remain
unmea√
sured. The one-day and thirty-day figures are T projections
from measured six-hour data and are labelled as such; a 20 h
coincident segment permits a direct check. Extracting a stable
offset at the D1 level requires the source-geometry model,
and the O3 run measured exactly the wander that model
must remove. The sequential-detection guarantee weakens
under flicker innovations (Remark 1). The 90 s floor awaits
its mechanism decision. None of these is hidden inside an
assumption; each is a named, testable route in Section V-D.
2) Sensor and deployment envelope: The natural field is 0.1
to 1 pT in band, and the limiting factor in deployment is rarely
the intrinsic noise floor of a modern induction coil. It is the
local environment: mains harmonics and switching electronics,
microphonics from wind, rain, or footfall, vibrating metal,
and proximity to power infrastructure. The two configurations
measured here bracket this envelope rather than sampling its
best case. Vault-sited coils resolved corrected γ 2 (f1 ) = 0.29.
Facility fluxgates inside an operating interferometer building,
an electromagnetic environment few industrial nodes will exceed for hostility, still carried a detectable channel at corrected
γ 2 = 0.0058, at a measured precision cost of roughly five
times at one day.
Site quality therefore buys precision; it does not gate
existence, down to at least the facility-noise level measured.
Below that level burial is real: the architecture is functional
today where Schumann observatories already operate and
in engineered quiet enclosures, and it is not yet a drop-in
attachment for an arbitrary urban node. The gap is engineering, not physics: reference-sensor differencing, adaptive
cancellation of tracked interferers, mains-harmonic notching,
mechanical isolation, and pre-deployment site characterization,
which is the same noise-spectrum measurement the detector’s
calibration requires.

Where mitigation fails, the semantics fail safe. Sudden
loss of cross-node coherence is itself an integrity event,
consequentially indistinguishable from jamming, and flags the
node as unverified rather than silently trusted. This also keeps
the monitor off the always-drop equilibrium of Mizrahi’s
game [1], in which a detector attains maximum payoff by
rejecting every exchange while leaving the node with no
time at all: here the reject condition is a measured physical
event carrying a null battery behind it, not a threshold on
a statistic whose false-alarm rate is set by network delay
variance. “Commodity sensor” is retained in its precise sense,
that no exotic instrument is required, and explicitly not in the
sense of any sensor at any site.

E. Conclusion
Two-way time transfer will remain rank deficient; the
question is where the missing information comes from. This
paper’s answer is a globally excited, spatially correlated field
whose realization rides inside authenticated records, out of
reach of transport delay. The contribution is not a competitive
accuracy figure but the existence of a measured bound for
a node class whose guaranteed-under-attack precision was
previously not derivable at all from the observables available
to it.
Sequential detection removes the incremental attacker’s
advantage whenever offset innovations are white or short-range
correlated: by Proposition 1 the bias achievable before detection scales as the cube root of the ramp rate and vanishes as
the attack slows. Under flicker-type innovations the advantage
is bounded rather than eliminated.
The coherence this architecture requires is no longer assumed. It is measured at 3002 km through the wrong sensor
and through the right one, it survived its null battery twice, and
the preregistered sensor-matching prediction was confirmed
at fifty times, inside its window of thirty to one hundred,
by an analysis whose hash was on public deposit before the
data were seen. The separately preregistered static-lag-stability
criterion was not met and is retained as a limitation rather than
retroactively relaxed.

B. Null and Surrogate Construction
Pre-freeze review changed four things. Each is recorded
because each corrected a weaker design.
The primary statistic is canonical, not best-pair. Selecting
whichever scalar pair produces the largest peak is pair selection under another name. Equation (6) is rotation-invariant and
fixed a priori; the O3 pair matrix shows why this mattered.
N1 is a block permutation, not a time shift. For a stationary
process a time shift multiplies the cross-spectrum by a unitmagnitude phase factor and leaves magnitude-squared coherence unchanged, so a shift null is not a valid collapse test for
this estimator at the population level. The frozen N1 splits the
L1 vector record into 64 s blocks and permutes block order,
applying the same permutation to both L1 axes, preserving
marginal spectra and within-site covariance while destroying
inter-site temporal correspondence. Twenty surrogates give
empirical p < 1/21.
The 60 s shift is reported and not scored, for the reason
above. This is a property of the estimator, not of the dataset,
so it applies to the pilot battery equally.
The D2b null uses the same four-pair statistic as the
observation. An earlier design built the null from one shifted
pair multiplied by four, a different statistic and therefore not a
null for it. The frozen version passes 19 block-permutation
surrogates through the identical four-pair construction per
window.

F. Known Defects in the Frozen Deposit
Recorded rather than corrected, because correcting them
would break the freeze.
The D2b pass line is stated two ways. The protocol defines

The scoring code implements the first. The ambiguity is
